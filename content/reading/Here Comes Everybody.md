## Why
As a companion for [Ulysses]({{< relref "reading/Ulysses.md" >}}).

## What I liked
The book gives a unitary view of _A Portrait_, [Ulysses]({{< relref "reading/Ulysses.md" >}}) and _Finnegans Wake_. It's beautifully written and quite short (which is a great quality). If one doesn't feel like reading Joyce, this book is excellent on its own right to get a sense of the complexity and depth of Joyce's works.

## What I disliked
Nothing really. The only observation is that this is an "introductory" companion, meaning that one probably needs [other secondary sources]({{< relref "reading/Ulysses Unbound.md" >}}) to help with [Ulysses]({{< relref "reading/Ulysses.md" >}}).
