## Why

I heard a [podcast with Daniel Kahneman](https://www.samharris.org/podcasts/making-sense-episodes/150-the-map-of-misunderstanding-from-the-archive) and I enjoyed it very much. I've been meaning to read "Thinking, Fast and Slow" for a while, but I got to it just now.

## What I liked

The book is very dense: there is just an outstanding amount of information. A full life of research, distilled for the layman. The writing style is clear and accessible, but never banal.

The idea of _System 1_ and _System 2_, cooperating and competing to reach a decision, reminded me of the [Crystal Society]({{< relref "reading/Crystal Society.md" >}}) book.

## What I disliked

The book is very research oriented, and that's its strength and weakness: I would have loved more personal stories or more philosophical takes.
