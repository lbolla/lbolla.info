## How AI Is Reshaping Human Reasoning and the Rise of Cognitive Surrender

The article that expands on [Thinking, Fast and Slow]({{< relref "reading/thinking-fast-and-slow.md" >}}), by adding another "system of thought": _System 3_. _System 3_ is defined as "artificial cognition that operates outside the brain".

The authors introduce the concept of _cognitive surrender_: adopting AI outputs with minimal scrutiny, overriding intuition (_System 1_) and deliberation (_System 2_).

The article is useful at introducing a framework to talk about the problem of cognitive surrender, but it fails to explain clearly why only an AI qualifies for System 3. It seems like any external influence, like a friend or a TV news, would qualify. E.g. when uncertain about a judgement, asking to a friend would tip the scale to go along with the friend's recommendation.

I think the distinction is one of _quantity_, which causes a _qualitative_ difference. An AI is more ubiquitous and poses less friction than asking to a friend. Therefore, it is more available and this change in "quantity" causes its users to surrender to it more easily.
