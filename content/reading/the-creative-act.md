## Why

I heard Rick Rubin on a [podcast](https://www.nytimes.com/2023/02/10/opinion/ezra-klein-podcast-rick-rubin.html) and it immediatly clicked.

## What I liked

It's a short book, but very _very_ dense. Each chapter, there are 78, is at most 3 pages long. The book is poetic, deep, insightful. It makes you want to be an artist.

Nothing so far. I'll have to re-read it.
